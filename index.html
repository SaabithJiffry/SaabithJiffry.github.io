Key Capabilities

Adaptive 4-Tier HUD + Secondary Mic Pill

Track Mic state (w/ CoreAudio voice activity bar & muted-voice warnings), Recording timer, Chat unread count, OBS Studio/Scene, and Live Stream/VCam health. Toggle an extra independent Primary Mic Pill anywhere on screen.

Director Deck, Timer & Stage Nudge

Switch OBS scenes, Pause/Split recordings, toggle Standby BRB curtains, run synchronized presenter countdowns, and push floating stage nudges across Host, Remotes, and Phones.

Session Timeline & Chapter Exports

Bounded 2,000-entry timeline logs every mic, scene, slide, and recording marker and exports directly to Video Editor CSV markers, Markdown tables, or YouTube chapters.

Classroom Discovery & Failover

Guided setup wizards, 1-tap Classroom LAN UDP discovery (:4457), QR pairing, DHCP IP-shift auto-follow, and seamless LAN ↔ WAN Relay failover.
